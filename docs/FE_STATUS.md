## Route guards (Screen Authorization Matrix, 2026-11-19)
Roles are the six codes in `core/role-codes.ts` (EMPLOYEE, DEPT_MANAGER, BRANCH_MANAGER, HR_OFFICER, VERIFIER, SYSTEM_ADMIN); there are no aliases. Every screen route has `authGuard`; role/permission-bound routes add `adminGuard`, `organizationGuard` or `permissionGuard(...codes)` (campaigns `CAMPAIGN_READ`, my-work / work-monitor `WORK_READ`, employees `EMPLOYEE_READ`, target-overview / target-allocation `TARGET_MANAGE`, kpi-definitions `KPI_MANAGE` or `KPI_APPROVE`, evaluation-profiles `KPI_READ`, branch-performance `CAMPAIGN_READ`). Branch-level users that open a department/personal evaluation screen are sent to `/branch-performance/evaluations`.
