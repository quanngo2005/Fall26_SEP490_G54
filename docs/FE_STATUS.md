## Route guards (Screen Authorization Matrix, 2026-11-19)
Roles are the six codes in `core/role-codes.ts` (EMPLOYEE, DEPT_MANAGER, BRANCH_MANAGER, HR_OFFICER, VERIFIER, SYSTEM_ADMIN); there are no aliases.
